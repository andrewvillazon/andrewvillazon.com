With 2024 well and truly over, it's time to take a look back at the year that was.

## Publishing

It was the quietest year yet for the blog with only a couple of articles published. Much of my time this past year was spent learning and growing professionally. 

The purpose of this blog is to share things I think others might find helpful. However, this past year, I haven't felt like I've had much to share.

Despite the quiet year, I managed to put out a couple of articles, in reverse chronological order:

* [Adding source file information to tables in Databricks](/adding-source-file-information-databricks)
* [My favourite Python resources](/best-python-resources)

## Open Source

A big chunk of 2024 was spent working on my [pbipy](https://github.com/andrewvillazon/pbipy) project, adding new features and expanding coverage of the [Power BI REST API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/rest/api/power-bi/). There's a few endpoints left to cover, which I'll continue to chip away at.

The library saw five new [releases](https://github.com/andrewvillazon/pbipy/releases) in 2024, and the same number of new contributors, which was great. It's fantastic to see the library bringing value and solving problems for others.

## Thoughts on 2025

With 2025 almost halfway done, a significant change for me involves switching from working in Databricks to Snowflake, which should provide me with more inspiration for writing.

Thanks for reading in **2024**!
